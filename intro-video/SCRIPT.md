# Voiceover script — AgentSwarm intro film v2

Engine: Kokoro-82M, local, offline. Voice `am_michael`. Chosen after
`npx hyperframes auth status` was relayed and the offline route was picked
deliberately.

**The rule this script obeys:** the voice carries the *argument*; the screen
carries *labels and data*. A line is never spoken and shown at the same time.
That is why frame 01 shows five status rows while the voice says something the
rows do not say.

Voice: instrument, not assistant. Flat, unhurried, no upsell. Never exclaims.

## Words the script refuses

No "effortless", "seamless", "powerful", "revolutionary", "supercharge",
"unleash", "game-changing". No exclamation marks. No second-person flattery.
The product's own voice document (`app/src/style.css` header) is the authority:
a flight deck does not say "I need you".
