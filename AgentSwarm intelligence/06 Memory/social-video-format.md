**The shared grammar:**
1. Portrait 4:5, built for a feed, not a website hero.
2. Top ~70%: ONE continuous, always-moving visual. Raw capture — a phone
   filming a laptop, a webcam — not a polished render. Authenticity is the look.
3. Bottom ~30%: a persistent headline that never moves or changes, big and
   bold, plus an optional small body paragraph. The viewer always knows what
   they are looking at even if they scrolled in late.
4. A live data overlay ON the visual (a tracking reticle with a running
   accuracy readout; a knowledge graph with lit nodes).
5. A small brand mark, and a tiny CTA ("SWIPE FOR MORE").
6. 10-21 seconds. No cuts at all.

**How to apply:** take the grammar, never the design. Their exact layouts,
highlight styles, watermarks and handles are those creators' identity — build
AgentSwarm's own expression of the same structure using its own tokens.

The natural AgentSwarm subject in this format is the muster rail: five lamps,
four green, one turning amber, with real elapsed timers running — an always-
moving visual with live data on it, which is exactly what both references have.
