# Conversation Arc — where Recall's record lives

Recall was not decided in sessions of its own. It was born mid-conversation, inside the
AgentSwarm arc, and its entire record so far lives there. This note exists so the vault
says where its conversation history is, rather than appearing to have none — and
nothing here is duplicated, because a copied session note would drift from the
original.

| What happened to Recall | Where it is recorded |
|---|---|
| Born from an unrelated "Obsidian killer" question during the concept reset — and immediately proved sharper than the question that produced it | AgentSwarm vault → `05 Sessions\S2 - The Concept Reset` (2026-07-30) |
| The claim tested against reality and killed twice; the signal probe replaced the hero demo; the PRAXIS boundary audit ruled the products siblings | AgentSwarm vault → `05 Sessions\S3 - Evidence Before Code` (2026-07-31) |

Paths: working copy at `E:\agent-swarm\AgentSwarm intelligence\05 Sessions\`, archive

## Why the record is shared

Both products came out of the same conversations, and both compete in the same
tournament week for the same build hours. Splitting the transcript record would have
invented a history that never happened. The measurements that are Recall's own —
the signal probe and the converger recon — live in this vault under `02 Research`,
cited from those shared sessions.

## When this changes

The moment Recall wins build hours and runs sessions in its own project directory,
those sessions land here as S1, S2, … and this note becomes their index.
