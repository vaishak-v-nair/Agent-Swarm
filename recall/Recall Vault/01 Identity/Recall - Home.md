# Recall 🧾

*Working name. Final name still to pick: **SHERD** · WHYBACK · CITEBOOK (all free on npm).*

**Answer with receipts.**

Your knowledge no longer arrives as notes you write. It leaves as exhaust from work you already did — AI conversations, commits, sessions. Recall refines that exhaust into knowledge cards you can ask questions of, and every answer cites the exact transcript line and the exact commit where it happened.

It beats **re-asking the model**, not note-taking. A fresh model can guess again. It cannot show you where you decided.

## Relationship to PRAXIS

Decided by audit on 2026-07-30: **siblings, not duplicates.**

- **PRAXIS proves** what the AI did — signed receipts, hash chains, a judge ruling claims true or false.
- **Recall remembers** why — extracts decisions and gotchas, searches them, answers with sources.
- Neither crosses the line. PRAXIS never becomes a search engine. Recall never signs or judges.
- Shared code (`recall-core`): transcript reader, redaction, vault writer, commit join — pulled out of PRAXIS, used by both.
